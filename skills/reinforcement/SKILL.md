# Reinforcement / التعزيز

## Mechanisms
- **Recognition:** monthly showcase of measured wins (hours saved, quality improved) with team names, not just individuals.
- **Measures:** add AI-enabled process metrics to team KPIs; managers review them monthly.
- **Embed:** update procedures, templates and onboarding so the AI way is the default way; retire the old path where possible.
- **Feedback loop:** pulse survey every quarter; usage retention monitored.
- **Ownership:** hand over to process owners and champions before the programme closes.

## Signals that reinforcement is working
Reinforcement score ≥ 3.5; retention30d stable or rising; frequency and depth rising; positive theme "productivity" growing.
