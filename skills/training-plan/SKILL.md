# Training & enablement / التدريب والتمكين

Train only after Awareness and Desire are in place; otherwise attendance is low and learning doesn't transfer.

## Paths
| Path | Audience | Content | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI basics & safe use | everyone | what AI can/can't do, accuracy checks, data rules, acceptable use | 60-min live or e-learning |
| Role-based | per department | 5–10 real tasks of that role, prompts, review steps | 2×90-min workshop on their own work |
| Champions | champions | advanced prompting, coaching peers, collecting wins | 4 sessions + community |
| Managers | people managers | coaching, protecting practice time, recognising use, handling fears | 90 min |

## Prompt library per department
`.cmo/drafts/prompts-<group>.md`: task · prompt (ar/en) · how to check the output · time saved estimate. Start with the processes in `processes.json`.

## Ability support
Office hours weekly · champion pairing · practice time protected by managers · quick-reference cards.

## Measures
Knowledge/Ability scores in the next pulse, reach and depth in usage, timing samples on trained processes. Training activities in the plan: `lever: "training"`, `adkar: ["knowledge"]` or `["ability"]`, with `groups`.
