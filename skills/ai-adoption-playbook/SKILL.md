# AI adoption playbook / دليل تبنّي الذكاء الاصطناعي

## Foundations (prepare)
- **Acceptable-use policy:** approved tools, data classes allowed (public/internal/confidential/restricted), human review required for outputs, disclosure rules, prohibited uses. Short (1 page) + FAQ, in Arabic and English.
- **Governance:** owner (CIO/CDO), legal/compliance review, incident path for wrong outputs or data leaks, shadow-AI policy.
- **Access:** licences, SSO, data connectors, sensitivity labels.
- **Use-case discovery:** workshops per department — list top 10 repetitive tasks, pick 3 quick wins with measurable time.

## Rollout
- Pilot (1–2 groups, champions-heavy) → wave 1 (high-readiness groups) → wave 2 (high-impact, lower readiness) with more coaching.
- Licence allocation fairness: explain criteria to avoid resentment.
- Champions: 1 per 20–30 people; monthly champion meetup; share prompts and wins.

## Enablement
Role-based training on real tasks; prompt library per department; office hours; manager guide; "AI week" showcase.

## Measuring
Baseline before launch: survey, usage (0), process timings. Then pulses, monthly usage, after-timings 6–10 weeks after training.

## Pitfalls
Training before desire · no policy → fear or shadow AI · counting licences as adoption · claiming savings without measurement · ignoring job-security fears · saved time absorbed by more work without recognition.

## Regional notes (GCC / Arabic)
Arabic-first materials and prompts; dialect examples; data residency and sector regulators (finance, health, government); Ramadan and fiscal-year peaks in the schedule; formal sponsorship from senior leadership carries high weight.
