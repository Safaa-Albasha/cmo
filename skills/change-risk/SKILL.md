# Change risk / مخاطر التغيير

People-side risk is high when a **large change** meets a **less ready organisation**.

## Change characteristics (rate 1–5)
Scope (how many people), disruption to daily work, job-content change, timeline pressure, technical maturity of the tool, data sensitivity.

## Organisational attributes (rate 1–5, 5 = favourable)
Sponsor commitment, leadership alignment, middle-manager support, past change success, change saturation (5 = low), culture of experimentation, communication channels.

## Reading
- Change average ≥ 3.5 and organisation average ≤ 2.5 → **high risk**: stronger sponsorship, slower rollout, more coaching, earlier resistance management.
- Both mid → **medium**: standard plan with pulses every 6 weeks.
- Small change, favourable org → **low**: lighter plan.

## AI-specific risks to check
Job-security fear · accuracy/hallucination incidents eroding trust · data leakage into public tools · shadow AI (unapproved tools) · uneven licence allocation creating unfairness · "AI did it" accountability gaps · regulatory constraints (sector rules, data residency).

Each high risk → a proposed activity (lever + ADKAR target + owner) via `approval-protocol`. Record the rating summary in the assessment report (`.cmo/reports/assessment-<date>.md`).
