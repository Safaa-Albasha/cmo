# Communications plan / خطة الاتصال

## Principles
- **Sender matters:** business reasons from senior leaders; personal impact ("what does it mean for me") from direct managers.
- **Match the barrier:** awareness → why; desire → benefits and fears addressed; knowledge → how and rules; ability → support available; reinforcement → recognition and results.
- Repeat key messages 5–7 times across channels; two-way channels (Q&A, office hours) beat broadcasts.
- Bilingual audiences: send both languages or the group's preferred one.

## Message template
Subject · why (1–2 lines) · what changes for you · what support exists · what we ask you to do (one action) · where to ask questions.

## AI-specific topics to cover
Acceptable use and data rules · job security stance · accuracy: always review outputs · where to get help · how success is measured · celebrating early wins with real numbers (from `value`).
