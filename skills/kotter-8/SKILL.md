# Kotter's 8 steps / خطوات كوتر الثماني

Based on John Kotter's 8-Step Process for Leading Change (© Kotter, Inc.); guidance here is original.

| # | Step | For AI adoption | Evidence it is done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create urgency · خلق الإلحاح | business case: competitors, cost of manual work, employee demand | sponsor narrative published; awareness ≥ 3.5 |
| 2 | Guiding coalition · التحالف القيادي | executives across functions + IT + legal + HR | named coalition (`sponsors.json`), meeting cadence |
| 3 | Vision & strategy · الرؤية | "AI as assistant for every employee", 3–5 measurable goals | vision statement, success metrics in project |
| 4 | Communicate the vision · توصيل الرؤية | leaders repeat it; channels per group; FAQ | comms plan executed; awareness rising |
| 5 | Empower action · التمكين | licences, policy, training, champions, remove blockers (data access, approvals) | knowledge/ability rising; reach rising |
| 6 | Short-term wins · الإنجازات السريعة | publish measured wins (hours saved per process) within 90 days | showcase done; value with 🟢 evidence |
| 7 | Sustain acceleration · مواصلة الزخم | scale use cases, new departments, advanced training | new use cases, depth rising |
| 8 | Anchor in culture · الترسيخ | KPIs, recognition, onboarding includes AI | reinforcement ≥ 3.5; retention stable |

From `/cmo:plan`: `kotter[].status` missing → propose activities for that step; steps 1–4 belong to *prepare*/*early manage*, 5–6 to *manage*, 7–8 to *sustain* (`framework-mapping`).

Common failures: declaring victory after training (step 6 without evidence), coalition only from IT, vision without measurable goals.
