# Sponsor roadmap / خارطة طريق الراعي

The primary sponsor is the strongest predictor of success: active, visible, and building a coalition of peers.

## Roadmap by phase
| Phase | Sponsor actions |
|---|---|
| Prepare | approve the case and vision; name the coalition; fund licences and enablement; announce AI policy |
| Manage | kickoff message (why now, what it means for jobs, what support exists); attend showcases; use AI publicly; remove blockers raised by the change manager; answer job-security questions honestly |
| Sustain | recognise teams with measured results; add AI goals to leaders' KPIs; review value report; decide scale-up |

## Coalition (`sponsors.json`, controlled)
`coalition[]`: `{ id, name, role, groups: [], commitments: [] }` — one per major function. `champions[]`: `{ id, name, group }` — at least one per impacted group (≈ 1 per 20–30 people).

## Messages
Drafts in the project language (and second language if mixed audience), 150–250 words, one call to action, address the top negative theme of the audience group. Sending is 🟡.

## Checkpoints
Monthly 30-minute sponsor review: readiness, barrier points, resistance hot spots, decisions needed (pending APR ids).
