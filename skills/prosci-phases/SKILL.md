# Three-phase change process / مراحل التغيير الثلاث

Inspired by Prosci's 3-Phase Process (Prosci® is a trademark of Prosci, Inc.; content here is original guidance).

## 1. Prepare approach / تحضير النهج
Goal: understand the change, the people and the risk before acting.
- Define the change (what, why now, scope, success metrics) and the sponsor.
- Impact assessment per group (`change-impact`), readiness and risk (`org-readiness`, `change-risk`).
- Build the coalition and sponsor roadmap (`sponsor-roadmap`).
- Governance for AI (acceptable-use policy, data rules, approved tools).
- **Baseline:** survey wave + process baselines + usage snapshot before launch.
- Exit: plan approved, baseline captured, sponsor committed, groups and champions named.

## 2. Manage change / إدارة التغيير
Goal: move each group through ADKAR with the five levers.
- Execute communications, sponsorship actions, training, coaching, resistance management.
- Pulse surveys every 6–8 weeks; monthly usage snapshots; timing samples on key processes.
- Adapt the plan to barrier points (`/cmo:plan` shows gaps).
- Exit: adoption targets reached for most groups; barrier points beyond Knowledge resolved.

## 3. Sustain outcomes / استدامة النتائج
Goal: make the change stick and prove value.
- Reinforcement: recognition, KPIs, manager follow-up, embed AI in processes.
- Value report (time, quality, ROI) with evidence; lessons learned; hand-over to operations.
- Exit: retention stable, reinforcement scores ≥ 3.5, value confirmed for key processes, ownership transferred.

Map every plan activity to exactly one phase (`phase` field).
